Practice Problems

  1. The design speed on a road is 60 kmph. Assuming driver reaction time = 2.5 sec and coefficient of friction = 0.35, calculate the required stopping distance for two-way traffic on a single lane road.
  2. Compute the perception-reaction time for a vehicle at 90 kmph, coefficient of friction = 0.35, and stopping sight distance = 170 m.
  3. Find the rate of superelevation for a horizontal curve of radius 500 m for a design speed of 100 kmph.
  4. The lateral friction mobilized for a curve radius = 400 m at design speed = 100 kmph is?
  5. The radius of a horizontal curve is 200 m, design speed 60 kmph, no superelevation. Find the required coefficient of friction.
  6. Find the safe overtaking sight distance for a design speed of 96 kmph (as per IRC).
  7. A valley curve has gradients of 1 in 40 (descending) and 1 in 50 (ascending). Find length for design speed 80 kmph (comfort condition).
  8. A valley curve with gradients 1/20 and 1/30, design speed = 85 kmph. Compute length for comfort and headlight distance. Assume rate of change = 0.61 m/s³.
  9. A summit curve must provide overtaking sight distance = 230 m. Gradients: +1/45 and -1/60. Design speed = 80 kmph. Find curve length.
  10. Find valley curve length for gradients +2% and -3%, design speed = 100 kmph, rate of change = 0.6.
  11. A crest curve joins +3% and -2%, design speed = 80 kmph, SSD = 120 m. Driver eye height = 1.2 m, object height = 0.15 m. Calculate curve length.
  12. A valley curve (GATE 2013): gradients 1 in 25 and 1 in 20, design speed = 90 kmph. Conditions: i) SSD = headlight distance; ii) comfort condition with rate = 0.5 m/s³. Reaction time = 2.5 sec, friction = 0.35, headlight height = 0.75 m, beam angle = 1°.
  13. SSD on a highway with ascending gradient of 3%, design speed = 60 kmph is …
  14. Car at 80 kmph on descending gradient of 4%, coefficient of friction = 0.35. Find minimum SSD.
  15. Car at 80 kmph, driver reaction time = 2.5 sec, coefficient of friction = 0.35. Find lag distance.
  16. Car at 80 kmph, reaction time = 2.5 sec, coefficient of friction = 0.4, brake efficiency = 50%. Find SSD.
  17. Intermediate sight distance required for design speed = 80 kmph, coefficient of friction = 0.36 is …
  18. Design a horizontal curve for plain terrain, design speed = 80 kmph, max superelevation = 0.07, lateral friction = 0.15. Find ruling minimum radius.
  19. Car at 40 kmph descending grade requires braking distance = 1.5 × ascending grade distance. Friction = 0.35. Find grade of road.
  20. Two-lane road with curve radius 450 m, design speed = 80 kmph, pavement width = 7.5 m. Find rise of outer edge when rotated with respect to centerline.
  21. Priority intersection: one-lane road (20 kmph) crosses two-lane road (50 kmph). Reaction time = 2.5s, friction = 0.38. Find minimum sight triangle lengths.
  22. A super-elevation e is provided such that a vehicle can stop without sliding. For design speed v and friction f, write the criterion.
  23. A two-lane one-way road (radius 50 m), lorry wheelbase = 5 m, speed 60–80 kmph. Compute mechanical widening and psychological widening.
  24. A curved portion: curve length = 200 m, radius = 300 m, pavement width = 7.5 m. Provide SSD = 80 m. Find setback distance.
  25. Formula: braking distance for speed v, slope G, friction fr.
  26. A road curve: deflection angle = 55°, radius = 250 m. Transition curve length required (rate = 0.3 m/s³, speed = 50 kmph).
  27. Vehicle at 60 kmph, ascending gradient. Ratio of lag to brake distance = 6:5. Reaction time = 2.5s, friction = 0.36. Find gradient (%).
  28. Car at 65 kmph, coefficient of friction = 0.396. Find braking distance.
  29. Traffic stream at 50 kmph, headway = 2.52 s. Find longitudinal spacing of vehicles.
  30. Two cars at 60 and 80 kmph approach head-on, friction = 0.35. Find minimum sight distance to avoid collision.
  31. Stopping sight distance definition: a) distance to stop vehicle without collision b) distance visible to driver at night c) height of object d) height of driver’s eye
